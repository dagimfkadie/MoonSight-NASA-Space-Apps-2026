## Verification record

For every AI-assisted implementation, record:
- File or feature affected.
- AI assistance provided.
- Human reviewer.
- Tests or checks actually run.
- Known limitations or unresolved issues.

## Media

Record any AI-generated or AI-edited images, video, audio, or other media before including them in the submission. Check the current competition rules and obtain required permissions and attributions.

## Accuracy commitment

AI assistance is not evidence that code works or that scientific results are correct. The team is responsible for reviewing and validating all submitted work. Do not describe AI-generated work as independently authored by a team member.
